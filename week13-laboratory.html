Evidence boundaries for biological claims

Relevant literature includes human-cell inflammatory response measurements (Vieira et al., 2023), human clinical studies of particular Echinacea preparations (Weishaupt et al., 2020; Lee et al., 2024), safety synthesis (Ogal et al., 2026), and fermentation studies (Rizzello et al., 2013). These datasets involve different materials, processes, doses, and endpoints.

Do not: infer enhanced phagocytosis from a UV chromatogram, equate suppression of IL-6 with clinical cytokine-storm prevention, or treat a fermentation hypothesis as a confirmed processing benefit.

References for independent student research: Vieira et al. (2023), doi:10.3390/antiox12020425; Rizzello et al. (2013), doi:10.1186/1475-2859-12-44; Weishaupt et al.
