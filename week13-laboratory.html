Evidence boundaries for biological claims

Use the assigned human-cell, human clinical, safety, and fermentation literature as separate evidence streams. Compare material, process, dose, and endpoint before applying published results to the proposed product.

Do not infer: enhanced phagocytosis from a UV chromatogram; clinical prevention of excessive inflammation from reduced IL-6 in a cell model; or a confirmed processing benefit from a fermentation hypothesis that has not been measured in the proposed product.
