Document your investigation — Week 10 Laboratory Record

This simulator is your laboratory record for Week 10. Enter your prediction, run the experiment, record your observation and next decision, and select Save annotated run for every informative run you want included. The saved record remains in this browser for this week. At the end of the investigation, select Create PDF Lab Record. Your browser will open its print/save interface; choose Save as PDF, choose where to save the file, and upload that PDF with the Week 10 Canvas assignment. Only saved runs are included in the PDF. Complete the week's laboratory work in the same browser/device until you have saved and submitted the PDF. Week 11 begins a new laboratory record.
